Game: Animal Ninja: Cat Roguelike (Android: com.bringnext.animalninja, iOS: com.aninja.game)
Developer: BringNext LLC (the name shown on our Google Play and App Store pages)
Last updated: October 19, 2026

You can ask us to delete your game account and the data linked to it at any time, free of charge. You do not need to reinstall the Game, and you can ask whether or not you still have it installed.

Animal Ninja: Cat Roguelike does not use usernames, passwords or email sign-in. Your "account" is the random Player ID the Game created on your device. That ID is linked to your cloud save, your in-game wallet, your purchase records and your gameplay statistics.

Before you delete: deletion is permanent. Your progress, heroes, items, Rubies, gold and other Virtual Items cannot be recovered, and we cannot refund them. Items the store records as permanent purchases, such as the No-Ads Pass or heroes bought with real money, can be restored later with Settings > Restore purchases using the same Google or Apple account.

Option 1: Delete from inside the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Animal Ninja: Cat Roguelike.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. Open Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete my data.
  5. Read the message ("This erases your save and your data from this device and our servers. This can't be undone."), then tap Delete to confirm.
  6. Keep your device online for a moment. If you are offline, the request is kept on your device and sent automatically the next time you open the Game with an internet connection.

The Game then returns to the home screen with a fresh start on this device and a new, unrelated Player ID.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (if you no longer have the Game)

  1. Send an email to contact@bringnextinc.com with the subject "Delete my data - Animal Ninja: Cat Roguelike".
  2. Include your Player ID, shown in Settings > Privacy in the Game (tap the copy button next to it).
    • If you no longer have the Game or cannot find your Player ID, tell us the store order number of any purchase you made (Google Play order number starting with GPA., or the Apple order ID from your receipt email), and the approximate date you last played. We use this to find the Player ID linked to that purchase.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request is genuine before deleting anything.

We confirm deletion by email within 30 days of receiving a verifiable request.

Email a deletion request

Our support page is https://bringnextinc.com/contact. Players under 13: a parent or guardian can make this request on the child's behalf using either option.

What we delete

Data What happens
Player ID Deleted from our servers.
Cloud save (progress, heroes, items, settings) and older copies of it Deleted.
In-game wallet (Rubies, gold and other balances) and its history Deleted.
Gameplay telemetry linked to your Player ID, and daily activity summaries linked to it Deleted. Statistics already combined across all players, which contain no Player ID, are kept because they no longer relate to you.
Bug reports you sent (description, screenshot, activity records) Deleted.
Data on your device (save, reminders, telemetry file) Removed when you use Option 1, or when you uninstall the Game.

What we keep, for how long, and why

Data How long Why
Purchase and transaction records: store transaction ID, product, price, currency, date, refund status 7 years from the purchase Tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of sales. After deletion your Player ID in these records is replaced by a one-way hash, so they are no longer linked to your game data, cloud save or telemetry.
Records held by Google Play or the App Store about your purchases Under Google's or Apple's own policies These are the stores' records, not ours, and are tied to your Google or Apple account. Contact them directly to manage them.
A record that a deletion request was received and completed: date, number of records removed, and a one-way hash of the Player ID 3 years To prove we honored your request.
Records needed for an ongoing fraud investigation, chargeback or legal claim Until the matter is resolved Legal obligation and legitimate interest in defending legal claims.
Database backups Up to 30 days Backups are encrypted, are not used for anything else, and are overwritten automatically on a rolling basis. Deleted data disappears when the backup that holds it expires.
Firebase Analytics events from your device Up to 2 months Google holds these under a Firebase app instance ID that we do not store and that is not connected to your Player ID, so we cannot look it up. It expires automatically.
Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics Up to 90 days Crash reports are not linked to your Player ID and expire automatically.

Data held by advertising partners

We do not store advertising IDs on our servers. To control the data Google uses for ads:

  • Android: go to your device's Google ads settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads; wording varies by device) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to stop the Game from tracking you.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in other places where a choice is required, you can also change your ad choices in the Game at Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Delete some data without deleting everything

You can also ask us, by email, to delete only certain data, for example a bug report you sent or your gameplay statistics, while keeping your cloud save. Write to contact@bringnextinc.com with your Player ID and tell us what you want deleted.
