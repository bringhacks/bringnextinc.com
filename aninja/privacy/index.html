This Privacy Policy explains how BringNext LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you play Animal Ninja: Cat Roguelike (the "Game") on Android or iOS, and what choices and rights you have.

We designed the Game to collect as little as possible:

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • We do not require you to create an account with a username or password.
  • We identify your copy of the Game with a random Player ID that is not your name or email.
  • Players under 13 get a more protective version of the Game (see Section 9).

If you have questions, contact us at contact@bringnextinc.com.

2. Information we collect

The following stays on your phone or tablet. We do not receive it unless it is part of a bug report you choose to send (see 2.2).

Information Why

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, postal address, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos from your library, microphone or camera input, or payment card details. We do not offer chat or let players publish content to other players.

4.1 Advertising in the Game

The Game shows rewarded ads only: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. There are no banner or forced interstitial ads. We do not use ad mediation networks.

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other places where Google requires it): we use Google's consent form (UMP) to ask whether you agree to personalized ads and analytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices. That row appears only where the law requires a choice.
  • iOS: players aged 16 and over see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, after a short screen that explains it. The IDFA is used only if you allow tracking. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings (wording varies by device, usually Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Under 16: ads are never personalized, in any country (see Section 9).
  • Under 13: ads follow Google's child-directed treatment and are limited to content rated G. Players 13 and over see ads limited to content rated T (teen) or below.

5. "Sale" and "sharing" under US state privacy laws (including California)

We do not sell personal information for money. However, when personalized ads are enabled, AdMob's use of the advertising ID and ad interaction data may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, or as a "sale" or "targeted advertising", under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws in other US states.

How to opt out ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"):

  1. In the Game, open Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices where it is shown, and choose not to have personalized ads.
  2. On your device, turn personalized ads off. On iOS, deny the tracking prompt or switch the Game off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, opt out of ads personalization or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google ads settings.
  3. Or email contact@bringnextinc.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share" and your Player ID. We will confirm your request and tell you how to complete the opt-out on your device.

Players we know to be under 16 never receive personalized ads, in any country, so we do not sell or share the personal information of players under 16. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would give you a right to limit it.

Categories collected in the last 12 months (CCPA terms): identifiers (Player ID, advertising ID, IP address); commercial information (purchase records); internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay events, ad interactions); geolocation (approximate country or region only, derived from IP address by Google); inferences (none for players under 16; for others, ad interests inferred by Google if personalized ads are on). For sources, purposes and recipients, see Sections 2 to 4.

7. Your rights and choices

7.1 Choices available to everyone

  • Delete your data in the Game: Settings > Privacy > Delete my data, or by email. See our Delete your data page.
  • Change ad privacy choices: Settings > Privacy > Privacy choices (shown in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where a choice is required).
  • Turn off reminders: Settings > Notifications, or through your device's notification settings.
  • Limit ad tracking: your device's advertising ID or tracking settings (Section 4.1).
  • Find your Player ID: Settings > Privacy shows your Player ID with a copy button. Include it in any request so we can find your data.

7.2 EU, EEA, UK and Swiss players

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability: receive your cloud save and related data in a machine-readable format (JSON);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).

7.3 California and other US state residents

Depending on your state, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, sell or share;
  • access and receive a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising (Section 5);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect any);
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights;
  • appeal our decision on your request, by replying to our answer with the subject "Appeal".

You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof of authorization.

7.4 How to make a request, and how we verify it

Email contact@bringnextinc.com with your request and your Player ID. Because we do not collect your name or email, the Player ID is how we find your data. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the Game to make sure it comes from the device that holds that Player ID.

We respond within one month (GDPR / UK GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA). Both deadlines can be extended where the law allows; we will tell you if that happens. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

8. International data transfers

We and our providers process information in several countries, including the United States (BringNext LLC, Google) and Singapore (our game servers). These countries may have data protection laws different from yours.

When we transfer personal data out of the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum or IDTA), included in our providers' data processing terms, or on the EU-US, UK-US and Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified.

9. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience, including children. The first time a run ends, the Game shows a neutral age screen: it asks only for a year of birth, pre-fills no year, and does not hint at a "right" answer. The year stays on the device. Ads do not start until the age is known.

9.1 Players under 13 (COPPA)

For players who tell us they are under 13, we follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable laws, and the Game behaves differently:

Area What changes for under-13 players
Ads Ads are never personalized. Requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and limited to content rated G. The advertising ID is not used for advertising, and the iOS tracking prompt is never shown.
Analytics Firebase ad personalization signals are turned off, and Firebase's advertising ID collection is off for everyone. We do not send an age group for these players. Data is used only to run and improve the Game, not to build profiles or for advertising. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics and crash reporting stay off for players under 16 because they cannot give consent.
Player ID and telemetry Used only to support the internal operation of the Game (saving progress, delivering purchases, preventing fraud, keeping the Game working, measuring which stages work). Never used to contact the child, for advertising or to build a profile.
Reminders Only "hearts are full" reminders are on by default. The other reminders ("come back" and daily reminders) stay off unless turned on in Settings > Notifications. They stay on the device.
Bug reports No free text is sent. The report contains only the screenshot, activity records, device model and language. We delete any personal information we find in a report.
Purchases Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, which let parents require approval (Google Family Link, Apple Ask to Buy). We encourage parents to turn this on.

Apart from these persistent identifiers used for internal operations, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Parents: you can ask us to review or delete your child's information, or to stop collecting it, by emailing contact@bringnextinc.com with your child's Player ID (shown in Settings > Privacy). You can also use Delete my data in the Game yourself. COPPA operator contact: BringNext LLC, 1021 E Lincolnway #9340, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States, contact@bringnextinc.com.

9.2 Players aged 13 to 15

Players under 16 also receive non-personalized ads only in every country, and in the EU, EEA and UK their ad requests are tagged as under the age of consent, so they are not asked to consent to personalized ads. Players aged 13 to 15 are not shown the iOS tracking prompt.

9.3 Players aged 16 and 17

Ads are limited to content rated T (teen) or below. In the UK we take the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code into account for players under 18: high-privacy settings by default, no nudges to weaken privacy settings, and no use of data in ways that are detrimental to their wellbeing.

10. Security

We protect information with measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all communication between the Game and our servers;
  • access to the database and admin panel restricted to authorized people, with strong authentication and lockout after repeated failed sign-ins;
  • separation of the admin API from the game API;
  • rate limiting and server-side validation of purchases and wallet changes;
  • regular, access-controlled backups.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the Game before it takes effect and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again. Previous versions are available on request.
