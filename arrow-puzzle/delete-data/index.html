Delete Your Data

Arrow Puzzle: Match 3 Color, by BringNext LLC

Arrow Puzzle: Match 3 Color has no accounts. Your progress is stored only on your device, and Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase collect device identifiers and gameplay events as described in our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open your Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Puzzle: Match 3 Color.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the Game.

This permanently deletes your progress, coins, boosters and settings. We cannot restore them.

2. Ask us to delete analytics data

  1. Email contact@bringnextinc.com with the subject "Delete my data – Arrow Puzzle".
  2. Tell us your device model and roughly when you last played, so we can find the records linked to your device.
  3. We reply by email and delete the Google Analytics for Firebase data we can link to your device within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game progress, coins, boosters, settings (on device)Deleted when you clear storage or uninstall.
Gameplay events and app instance ID in Google Analytics for FirebaseDeleted within 30 days of your request. Otherwise kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.
Advertising data held by Google AdMobControlled by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and see Google's ads policies.
Your email to usKept only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted.
