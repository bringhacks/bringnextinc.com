Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle: Match 3 Color

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BringNext LLC ("we", "us") and the services we use collect when you play Arrow Puzzle: Match 3 Color (the "Game") on Android, and what choices you have.

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • Your progress, coins, boosters and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how levels are played. These services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

Questions: contact@bringnextinc.com.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by BringNext LLC, 1021 E Lincolnway #9340, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States. For the EU and UK GDPR, we are the controller of the personal data described here. Website: https://bringnextinc.com.

3. How we use information and our legal bases

  • Providing and improving the Game (analytics): our legitimate interest in making the Game better, or your consent where the law requires it.
  • Showing ads, which keep the Game free: your consent where required (for example in the EEA and UK, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest.

4. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: how Google uses information in ads
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Firebase privacy information
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Under some US state laws (including California), letting ad partners use device identifiers for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "selling". You can opt out at any time with the device setting in Section 6.

5. How long information is kept

On-device data is kept until you delete the app or clear its storage. Firebase Analytics data is kept for 14 months. AdMob data is kept according to Google's policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the Game to remove all on-device data. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email contact@bringnextinc.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use these rights.

7. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
