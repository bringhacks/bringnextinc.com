1. Who we are

Pearlwave (the "Game") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS. It is published by BringNext LLC, 1021 E Lincolnway #9340, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States ("we", "us").

Contact for anything in this policy: contact@bringnextinc.com.

2. The short version

  1. The Game has no account, no sign in and no server of ours. Your progress, settings and purchase records are stored on your device only.
  2. The Game is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and it does not ask your age (section 4).
  3. The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, and short full screen ads between some levels (section 5.2). The advertising company that serves them receives some technical data from your device (section 5). Where the law requires your permission, ads are personalized only if you allow it; if you refuse, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  4. Where the law lets us, and only after you accept where the law requires your consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game uses Google Firebase to send usage and crash data and to receive small remote settings for level difficulty (section 5.3). The data does not name you, and we do not use it for advertising.
  5. We do not sell your data. We do not run banner ads. We do not sell random items. The Game has a free reward chest whose content is random among items you have already unlocked, the chances are shown before you open it, and it cannot be bought with money or in game currency.
  6. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the Game asks for your consent before ads, usage data, crash reports or remote settings start, and on iOS it asks once for tracking permission. You can change these choices later (sections 5.4, 5.5 and 7).

3. What is stored on your device

The following is created by the Game and stays on your device. We do not receive it.

Data What it is used for
Game progress: levels, stars, items, in game currency ("Shells"), collection, statistics, daily challenge and calendar state Letting you continue where you stopped
Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, text size, reduce motion, colour symbols, hints Remembering your choices
Purchase records: which products you own, and a log used so a reward is never granted twice Giving you what you bought, and restoring it
Your consent choice and whether the Game already asked for tracking permission Not asking again, and respecting your choice
Scheduled reminder notifications (only if you allow them) Reminding you, for example that your lives are full. These are created on your device, not by a server

You can erase all of this at any time: Settings, Privacy, "Delete local data". Uninstalling the Game also removes it. Erasing local data also tells Firebase to reset the analytics identifier on your device, deletes crash reports that have not been sent, and asks Firebase to delete the installation identifier and the stored remote settings (section 5.3). It does not delete data that an advertising or analytics provider has already received from your device. Section 7 explains how to ask for that.

If your device backs up app data (for example Android Auto Backup or iCloud), your save file may be included in that backup by your operating system. We do not control or see those backups.

4. Who the Game is for

The Game is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. The Game does not ask your age, so it does not change ads, purchases or data collection by age: every player gets the same features.

Today the Game does not collect personal data on any server of ours. The only data that leaves your device goes to the advertising and analytics services described in section 5. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you learn that a child under 13 has sent data through the Game, write to contact@bringnextinc.com. We will delete what we hold and ask the providers named in section 5 to delete the rest.

Parents can restrict purchases and ads on a child's device using the controls of Google Family Link or Apple Screen Time.

5. Information that leaves your device

The Game does not need an internet connection to be played. Information leaves your device only in the cases below.

5.1 Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells the Game which product you bought, and the Game checks the receipt on your device. We do not run a server that stores purchases. Read the privacy policy of your store: Google Play https://policies.google.com/privacy, Apple https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5.2 Ads

The Game uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads. Rewarded video ads are shown only when you tap a button that names the reward. Interstitial ads are short full screen ads between levels, and you do not choose them. At the time of writing they start from level 16 and from your second session, appear after every third win, come at least 3 minutes after the previous full screen ad and at most 6 times a day, and never appear after a loss or right after a rewarded ad. They are not shown if you bought the ad free purchase, nor in the 7 days after any purchase. Each ad is preceded by a short "be right back" screen and followed by a thank you screen, which are part of the Game. There are no banner ads and no ads at launch.

When an ad is requested, Google may receive and use data from your device, such as your IP address, device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier where permitted), device model and operating system, app version, language, and information about how you interact with ads (impressions, taps, video views). Google uses it to select and show ads, measure them, limit how often you see an ad, prevent fraud and meet its legal duties. On Android the Game declares the advertising ID permission (AD_ID), so Google receives the advertising ID with ad requests also when ads are not personalized, for measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention. We ask Google to limit ads to the content rating PG or lower, and we do not mark the Game or its ad requests as directed to children. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Personalized ads use your advertising identifier. They are shown only where the law allows it and, where the law requires your permission (the consent message in section 5.4 and the iOS tracking permission in section 5.5), only if you give it. Without permission you still see ads, but they are not personalized, and Google may still use the identifiers described above for measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

5.3 Usage, crash and remote settings data (Google Firebase)

The Game uses three services of Firebase, a Google product: Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, California, USA) provides them, and Google Ireland Limited provides them to users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Google processes this data for us as our service provider (processor) under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms. See also https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The three services stay switched off until the consent step of the Game has run. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), nothing is sent until you accept, and nothing is sent if you refuse or have not answered. Where consent is not required, they start without a question. In Settings, Privacy, the switch "Share usage data" lets every player, not only those who see the consent message, turn them off at any time; turning it off takes the same road as withdrawing consent (the analytics data on the device is reset, unsent crash reports and the Firebase installation identifier are deleted).

  1. Firebase Analytics. The Game sends events that describe how it is played: the first launch and steps of the first minutes, levels started and finished (level number, mode, win or loss, stars, moves used and left, goals left, boosters and items used, moves bought with in game currency, and the difficulty setting from Remote Config described below), screens opened (map, shop, aquarium, daily challenge), in game currency earned or spent, decorations and rewards, weekly goals, notification and reminder choices (for example whether the permission was granted, and which reminder opened the Game), ads offered and rewarded (the placement only), a purchase completed (the product name only, never your payment details), settings changed (the name of the setting), and the length and number of sessions. The Game also sets a few user properties: days since installation, highest level won, whether you have bought something, your notification permission state and test group. Firebase adds technical data by itself: device model, operating system and app version, language, the country it derives from your connection, the time of the first launch, and engagement time. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We do not turn on Google signals, we do not use or collect the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier for Analytics, we do not link Analytics with Google Ads or other advertising products, and we do not set a user ID. The consent signals for advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization are set to denied.
  2. Firebase Crashlytics. When the Game crashes or hits an unhandled error, Crashlytics sends the stack trace, the state of the app at that moment, device details such as model, operating system version, memory and storage, a Crashlytics installation identifier (a random number made by the SDK) and the Firebase installation identifier. For some native crashes a memory dump is read temporarily to build the report, then discarded. Crash reports that are stored on your device but not yet sent are deleted if you refuse, withdraw consent or use "Delete local data".
  3. Firebase Remote Config. When the Game starts, and at most once every 3 hours, it asks Firebase for a few settings. The request contains the Firebase installation identifier, the app name and version, the language, the country code, the time zone, the operating system version, the version of the SDK and, because Analytics is on, the user properties above and the time of the first launch. Your IP address reaches Google as it does for any internet request. We use Remote Config for two things only: to change the number of moves of a campaign level by a small fixed amount (to balance difficulty from what players do in aggregate), and to switch a feature off if it causes a problem. It cannot switch a new feature on, change a price, or show you different content because of who you are. The settings are saved on your device and reused until the next successful request.
  4. Firebase installation identifier. Firebase creates a random identifier for each installation of the Game. It does not name you and Google says it does not uniquely identify a person or a physical device. Crashlytics and Remote Config use it. If you refuse or withdraw consent, or use "Delete local data", the Game asks Firebase to delete it and removes the stored settings. Google then removes the data tied to it from its live and backup systems within 180 days. A new identifier is created only after consent is given again.

We use this data to find and fix bugs, to make levels fairer and to improve the Game. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location, and we do not use this data for advertising.

5.4 Consent tool

In some regions (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the Game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads or analytics start. Each time you open the Game it checks whether your consent information needs an update, and it shows the message again only when the rules of your region require it. Ads are requested only after the consent step has run. If you accept, ads can be personalized. If you refuse, you still see ads, but the Game marks the requests as non personalized (section 5.2). If the consent information cannot be loaded, for example because you are offline, the Game does not request ads until it can. Usage data, crash reports and remote settings (section 5.3) follow your choice: if you refuse, they are not sent and no remote settings are requested. Google keeps your choice on your device so you are not asked at every launch. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy, "Ad choices", where that option is required.

5.5 iOS tracking permission

On iOS the Game asks once, before the first ad is requested, whether it may use your advertising identifier to show you personalized ads, which Apple calls tracking (the App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you say no, iOS does not give the Game your advertising identifier and ads are not personalized. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you may see both this prompt and the consent message in section 5.4. They are separate choices. iOS shows its prompt only once per installation, and you can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. The Game records only that it has already asked (section 3).

5.6 Reviews

The Game may ask the store to show its standard rating sheet once. The store, not the Game, handles your rating and any text you write.

5.7 Links

Links to this policy, our terms and support open in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

6. What we do not do

  1. We do not ask you to create an account.
  2. We do not sell your personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
  3. We do not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, calendar or precise location.
  4. We do not send promotional notifications. Reminder notifications never advertise purchases.
  5. We do not ask your age and we do not keep an age setting.
  6. We do not use Google signals or an advertising identifier for Firebase, and we do not link Firebase with advertising products (section 5.3).

7. Your choices and rights

  1. Delete local data. Settings, Privacy, "Delete local data" erases your progress and settings on the device, resets the Firebase analytics identifier, deletes unsent crash reports, and asks Firebase to delete the installation identifier (section 5.3). It does not erase data that ad or analytics providers already hold; for that, use item 5.
  2. Ads. Where a consent message is shown you can accept, refuse or change your choice in Settings, Privacy, "Ad choices". On iOS you can refuse tracking in the system prompt and change it later in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  3. Notifications. Reminders are off until you allow them. You can switch them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.
  4. Usage data. Where the law requires consent, Firebase usage data, crash reports and remote settings are not collected until you accept, and they stop if you refuse or withdraw consent. When you withdraw, the Game also deletes the local copies and asks Firebase to delete the installation identifier (section 5.3). You can withdraw consent in Settings, Privacy, "Ad choices", where that option is shown, or with the switch "Share usage data" in the same screen. You can also stop all of it by uninstalling the Game.
  5. Your legal rights. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Brazil, California and other US states) you may have the right to ask about, access, correct, delete, port or object to the use of personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not know who you are from the Game, so to answer a request we may need you to give us the "Support ID" shown in Settings, Privacy (it is the Google Analytics app instance identifier of your installation, which Google can use to delete the analytics data tied to it; it appears only while usage data is shared) or other facts that let us find your records, and we may be unable to find any. Write to contact@bringnextinc.com. We answer within 30 days.
  6. Do Not Sell or Share (California and some other US states). Personalized advertising by our ad provider may count as "sharing" or targeted advertising under US state laws. Use the privacy choices option in Settings, Privacy, or write to us, to opt out.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where data leaves your device, we rely on: consent for personalized ads and for non essential usage analytics (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the ePrivacy rules on accessing information on your device); for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, which read and store an identifier on your device, the Game applies the same consent to all three, so we rely on consent for them everywhere consent is required, and we do not rely on legitimate interest for them; and the performance of a contract for purchases, which the store handles.

9. International transfers

Google processes Firebase data in the United States and other countries where Google or its subprocessors operate. Google states that it complies with the EU US and Swiss US Data Privacy Frameworks and the UK Extension to the EU US framework, and it also uses Standard Contractual Clauses in its Data Processing Terms. Google lists the subprocessors of Firebase at https://firebase.google.com/terms/subprocessors. Other providers named in section 5 may also process data in the United States and other countries; they rely on the EU US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. How long data is kept

  1. On your device: until you delete local data or uninstall.
  2. Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for 14 months, the longest period Google offers for this kind of property. Reports that Google builds from it and that do not contain an identifier may be kept longer.
  3. Firebase Crashlytics: crash data is kept for 90 days, after which Google starts removing it from its live and backup systems.
  4. Firebase installation identifier and Remote Config data: until the identifier is deleted by the Game (when you refuse or withdraw consent, or use "Delete local data"); Google then removes the data tied to it within 180 days. If the Game is uninstalled without that, Google deletes an installation identifier after a period of inactivity (270 days at the time of writing).
  5. Ad provider data: kept under the provider's own policy, linked in section 5.2.

11. Security

Data sent by the SDKs listed above travels over encrypted connections (Firebase uses HTTPS). Local save data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is not encrypted beyond what your operating system provides. Keep your device protected with a lock screen.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we update the date above and, where required, tell you in the Game.
