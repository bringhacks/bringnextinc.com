Privacy Policy — Jam Out: Train Escape Puzzle

Effective date: October 22, 2026 (applies from app version 1.1.0)

Jam Out: Train Escape Puzzle ("Jam Out", the "App") is a puzzle game for iOS and Android built and operated by Bring Next LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains what happens to information when you play it. It applies specifically to Jam Out — for our other apps, see our general Privacy Policy.

In short: from version 1.1.0, Jam Out collects anonymous gameplay statistics so we can balance its puzzles. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID, it has no ads, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 collected no data at all.

Information We Collect

Jam Out has no accounts or sign-in, and the game has no text fields, so you cannot type anything into it that would be sent. The game plays offline: the information below is kept in a queue on your device and sent when your device is online. It is not linked to your identity.

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to understand how the game is played. It receives:

  • identifiers created for this install of the App: a random app-instance identifier and a Firebase installation identifier; on iPhone and iPad, Google Analytics also uses Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier that is the same for all apps from one developer on your device. None of these is your advertising ID;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, app version;
  • gameplay events: screens viewed, levels started and finished (with time, stars and hearts left), coins and gems earned and spent in the game, boosters and features used, settings changes;
  • session information: when the App is first opened, when a session starts and how long it is used, and when the App or the operating system is updated (on Android, also when the App's data is cleared or the App is removed);
  • on Android, the Google Play install referrer, which tells us which store page or link an install came from;
  • an approximate location (such as country, region or city) that Google derives from your IP address; Google does not store the IP address itself.

The App does not request or send the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, the advertising ID on Android), does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and switches off advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization in Google Analytics. You can read how Google handles this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Our game statistics service

The App also sends the same gameplay events to our own statistics server so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy. Each request contains a random install identifier created when you first open the App, the platform, whether the copy is a test version, app and operating system versions, device model, device language, the time it was sent, the gameplay events (each with its time) and, when you leave the App, a summary of your in-game statistics (for example levels completed, stars, streaks, coins and gems, trains owned, play time). Our server derives your country from the request's IP address and does not store the IP address; it uses the IP address only briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one device can send and to recognise Google's automated test devices (requests from the address ranges Google publishes for Firebase Test Lab, which tests every build uploaded to Google Play), so that they are left out of our statistics. Like any web service, our hosting provider may keep the IP address in its own short-lived network logs.

How We Use This Information

Only to run and improve Jam Out: to balance levels and rewards, find problems and measure which features players use. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we share it only with the service providers that process it for us: Google LLC for Firebase Analytics and Railway Corporation, which hosts our statistics server. These providers may process the information in the United States or other countries outside the one you live in.

Your Choices

The App has no setting that turns the statistics off. Uninstalling the App stops all collection. Notifications and links are covered below.

Retention and Deletion

Google Analytics keeps user-level and event-level data for the retention period set in our Firebase project, at most 14 months; after that only aggregated reports remain. Our statistics server keeps gameplay statistics for as long as we need them to balance and improve the game, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Because the information is not linked to your identity, we cannot look it up by your name or email, and version 1.1.0 does not show its install identifier, so we cannot reliably find the statistics of one particular device. If you would like us to try, email us with your device model, the app version and the approximate date you installed the App, and we will delete any records we can match to it. A new install usually starts with a new random identifier; restoring your device from a backup can bring the previous one back.

Information Stored on Your Device

To save your game, the App keeps a small file on your device with your progress (levels completed, stars, coins and gems, trains and themes you own, streaks, and settings such as sound and vibration). The file itself never leaves your device through the App; only the statistics summary described above is sent. Deleting the App deletes this file. Your operating system's own backup (for example iCloud or Android Auto Backup) may include it; such backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account.

In-Game Currency

Coins and gems are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the App. Jam Out has no in-app purchases: nothing in the App can be bought with real money.

Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example when a new daily puzzle is ready, your streak is about to end or a holiday event starts). They are created and delivered entirely on your device; no push service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your device settings.

Links to Other Websites

The settings screen can open our website (this policy, our terms and our contact page) and the app store page in your browser or store app, only when you tap them. Those pages are covered by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Jam Out is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; the anonymous statistics described above are the same for every player.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
