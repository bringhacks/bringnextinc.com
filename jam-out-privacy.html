Privacy Policy — Jam Out: Train Escape Puzzle

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Jam Out: Train Escape Puzzle ("Jam Out", the "App") is an offline puzzle game for iOS and Android built and operated by Bring Next LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains what happens to information when you play it. It applies specifically to Jam Out — for our other apps, see our general Privacy Policy.

In short: Jam Out does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

None. Jam Out has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party software development kits that collect data. The Android version does not request internet access.

Information Stored on Your Device

To save your game, the App keeps a small file on your device with your progress (levels completed, stars, coins and gems, trains and themes you own, streaks, and settings such as sound and vibration). This file never leaves your device through the App and we cannot access it. Deleting the App deletes this file. Your operating system's own backup (for example iCloud or Android Auto Backup) may include it; such backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account.

In-Game Currency

Coins and gems are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the App. Jam Out has no in-app purchases: nothing in the App can be bought with real money.

Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example when a new daily puzzle is ready or your streak is about to end). They are created and delivered entirely on your device; no push service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your device settings.

Links to Other Websites

The settings screen can open our website (this policy, our terms and our contact page) and the app store page in your browser or store app. Those pages are covered by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Jam Out is designed for players aged 13 and over. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@bringnextinc.com.
